About The Corner Brew

The Corner Brew is a small independent coffee shop serving speciality coffee, fresh pastries, light lunches, and homemade cakes. We focus on simple ingredients, friendly service, and a welcoming space where people can slow down and enjoy good coffee.

Whether you are grabbing a flat white before work, meeting a friend for brunch, or settling in with your laptop for an afternoon, our shop is designed to feel relaxed, warm, and comfortable.
